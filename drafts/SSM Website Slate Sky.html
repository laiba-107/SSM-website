§ 01
The Company

A trusted standard,
quietly held.

Security Systems Management Inc. is a minority-owned protective services firm headquartered in Hayward, California, with active operations across the United States and the Fiji Islands.

Speak with leadership

For more than a decade, SSM Inc. has delivered personnel security and facilities management services to federal, commercial, industrial and residential clients — building custom programs to the exact specifications of each engagement.

From the Greater Bay Area, the firm has expanded into the Washington D.C. metropolitan corridor and is positioned to deliver internationally. Our diversification of service offerings, paired with a dedicated management team and a documented record of excellent past performance, allows us to serve a wide range of clients without sacrificing the standards that built the practice.

We operate 24 hours a day, seven days a week. Our officers and patrols are bonded, background-checked, drug-tested, and trained at our own accredited academy — and our dispatch desk is staffed at every hour to give clients a real person on the line.
